# 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Build a complete, modern, professional personal portfolio website under the brand name **THE CODEFATHER**.

The project must include three integrated parts:

1. **Public Website:** A premium, responsive portfolio presenting personal information, skills, education, projects, and contact details.
2. **Admin Dashboard:** A secure control panel for managing all website content and settings without editing source code.
3. **Backend & Database:** A reliable backend connected to MySQL, handling authentication, content management, publishing, media, messages, notifications, backups, and all administrative operations.

This must be a functional, production-ready application, not just a static design or frontend prototype.

1. Administrator opens `/admin/login`.
2. Enters username or email.
3. Enters password.
